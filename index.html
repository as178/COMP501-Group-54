A2 Group 54's Website Submission:

Recommendation Algorithms in the Digital World &

their Effects on Society and Adolescents

This website's purpose is to present research regarding recommendation algorithms used in today's modern world digital systems. The presented research strongly focuses on the algorithms' frequently utilised recommendation, personalisation and tailoring capabilities, which affect the global society and majorly impact adolescents in particular.

As a group, we have chosen to do our research on this particular topic due to its increasing relevance in today's digital world and society as a whole. The majority of people utilising the Internet are exposed to recommendation algorithms and are influenced by their capabilities on a regular basis, including us. Upon researching what topic we should focus on, we discussed how each of us has had experiences where we individually encountered these algorithms being utilised on social media platforms, e-commerce platforms or even its influence on advertisements we were seeing on a day-to-day basis.

This prompted extensive discussions within our group, regarding the immense impact recommendation algorithms have on our lives. From the similarly tailored content we were seeing, to the targeted ads we were getting while browsing online, we noted that these algorithms are truly everywhere, affecting the way we feel, think and even our perspectives on a wide range of topics we discussed. From these conversations, we felt as if conducting research about these algorithms would not only benefit our own understanding of their implications on us as individuals, but also wider implications on society as a whole.

As young individuals ourselves, we initially wondered which demographic recommendation algorithms would affect the most. Upon further reflection, we decided to revolve our research around adolescents, as they make up a vast majority of online users today and interact with a variety of online platforms and digital systems, many of which have recommendation algorithms influencing their choices, decision-making capabilities and emotions. We collectively agreed that this can pose a lot of questions regarding adolescents' personal development, as well as how these algorithms could have a role in shaping such an impressionable demographic, and potentially future generations ahead.
